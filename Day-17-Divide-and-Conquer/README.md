# 📖 What is Divide and Conquer?

Divide and Conquer is an algorithmic technique that solves a problem by:

1. **Divide** the problem into smaller subproblems.
2. **Conquer** each subproblem recursively.
3. **Combine** the results to obtain the final solution.

Many efficient sorting and searching algorithms are based on this technique.

# 📊 Merge Sort vs Quick Sort

| Feature      | Merge Sort | Quick Sort 
|--------------|------------|------------
| Best Time    | O(n log n) | O(n log n) 
| Average Time | O(n log n) | O(n log n) 
| Worst Time   | O(n log n) | O(n²) 
| Extra Space  | O(n)       | O(log n) (recursion stack) 
| Stable       | ✅ Yes     | ❌ No 
| In-place     | ❌ No      | ✅ Yes
